# Hotel payout preparation and external payment recording

Admin page: `/admin/payouts` (Hotel Payouts in navigation and accounting). A hotel can be selected directly with `/admin/payouts?propertyId=<id>`. Owner history: `/partner/payouts`, using the existing property selector. Staff have neither history access nor payout commands.

## Simplified interface

The admin workspace shows three summaries: Ready to pay (unreserved final payable), Awaiting eligibility (the count of enrolled bookings waiting for checkout or their waiting period), and Paid to date (recorded external payments). Awaiting eligibility uses a booking count so guest collections are not added to net hotel payable. Prepared payouts have a separate contextual banner.

Tabs separate Ready bookings, Needs attention and History. Booking search, plain-language statuses and expandable breakdowns keep the default view focused on the next action. The schedule, minimum payout and pending collection details are behind a disclosure; the preparation footer also explains a missing minimum or a selected total below it. Ready booking assessments can be edited from View details. Owner history uses the same visual styling without admin controls.

## Workflow

1. Complete and reconcile booking accounting. A booking must be accounting-enrolled, have successful validated checkout proof and a recorded `actualCheckOutAt`, reliable collections, reconciled recognition and summary, and a positive outstanding hotel payable. Pending processing, unresolved refunds, cancellations and unsupported commercial changes block it. Verified separately paid services and fully reconciled service refunds participate in settlement under saved terms.
2. Review the booking's online collections, hotel collections, earned commission, and payable before adjustments. Commission has already been allocated; do not subtract it again. The saved booking delay controls eligibility in elapsed 24-hour days from actual checkout. Current delay changes do not change existing bookings.
3. In **Needs attention**, open **Review deductions**. All four categories begin Not reviewed: commission tax, statutory deductions, gateway fees, and other adjustments. An active admin must choose Enter amount, Confirmed ₹0, or Not applicable, with a reason for each decision. The assessment form accepts rupees with up to two decimal places and converts them to integer paise for the existing API. Amounts must be explicitly entered, including zero; blank inputs are not treated as zero. These are manual assessments, not tax calculations or remittance instructions. The administrator is responsible for substantiating the amounts and reasons from booking-specific evidence.
4. Select whole bookings and choose **Review payout**. The server previews the selected bookings, category deductions, final payment and minimum without reserving anything. **Confirm preparation** submits the preview fingerprint; changes to evidence, assessment versions, selection or preparation policy require a new review. Each booking needs a current complete assessment and positive final payment. The selected combined payment after deductions must meet the hotel's current configured minimum. At most 50 bookings can be included; prepare another batch for the remainder. Current minimum, cycle, timezone and policy revisions are saved on preparation; delay and commission remain the booking's saved terms. Cycle dates are guidance, permitting preparation outside the schedule. Missing cycle/timezone settings show configuration issues rather than an invented schedule.
5. A Prepared payout reserves each entire booking payable. Its exact payment, gross payable and deductions remain visible. Preparation posts no monetary journal entries. An unpaid payout can be cancelled with a reason, even if its source evidence changed or disappeared. Cancellation releases its reservations and preserves the payout and lifecycle history.
6. In **History**, open **Record payment** on a Prepared payout. After making a payment externally, enter payment date, method, transaction reference (required for every method), and optional notes. The hotel timezone determines whether the date is in the future. **Review payment** displays hotel, payout reference, bookings, exact amount and payment details; **Confirm recorded payment** records it. This does not initiate a transfer. Paid records cannot be edited or cancelled.

Changed evidence or an outdated assessment can prevent recording an external payment that has already been made. Review the accounting discrepancy; the system never silently drops a booking or changes a Prepared amount. Cancellation can release an unpaid operational reservation, but is not a reversal of an external transfer.

## Accounting and storage

Private collections: `hotelPayoutsV1` (with append-only `history`), `hotelPayoutAssessmentsV1` (with immutable `versions`), deterministic booking-level `hotelPayoutReservationsV1`, and `hotelPayoutRequestsV1`. Lifecycle statuses are only Prepared, Paid and Cancelled; evidence changes produce separate review warnings. Payout booking snapshots preserve evidence fingerprints, assessment versions, collection and commission breakdowns, amounts and saved booking terms.

Each command checks active-admin authorization transactionally. Preparation and payment reread booking/source evidence, journal, accounting state, assessments and reservations before any writes. Reservation ownership and exact prepared amounts must still match at payment. Request IDs are scoped to actors and payload fingerprints include the property and complete command. Identical retries return committed results; conflicting reuse fails. The UI retains a failed request ID for retries of the same action.

Payment atomically consumes reservations, writes Paid/history/request receipt, appends stable per-booking accounting events and updates journal-derived booking summaries. Each event debits gross hotel payable and separately credits external payment clearing and the four deduction clearing categories. No hotel dues, unrelated booking offsets, credits, tax or remittance policies are invented. Reprocessing unchanged accounting excludes payout events from collection source checks and preserves the reduced payable. Reconciliation validates recorded payout event references, immutable payment breakdowns and consumed reservations; summary rebuilding includes payment events.

If financial evidence changes after payment, the immutable payment and assessment history remain. Existing accounting processing reverses affected recognition with linked entries; any resulting debit hotel payable requires review. It is not automatically converted to hotel dues or used to offset another payout. Paid history shows a separate evidence warning.

Admin APIs: GET `/api/admin/payouts` (hotel directory, optional `after`) or `?propertyId=<id>` (hotel workspace); GET `/api/admin/payouts/<payoutId>?propertyId=<id>`; POST `/api/admin/payouts/commands?propertyId=<id>`. Command actions are `assess`, `prepare`, `cancel`, and `pay`, each requiring UUID `requestId`. Assessment also requires `expectedVersion`. Owner-only GET APIs: `/api/partner/properties/<propertyId>/payouts` and `/payouts/<payoutId>`. API responses use existing private/no-store handlers. Existing catch-all Firestore rules deny browser reads/writes to all payout data. Customer booking DTOs include an audience-safe payment summary; private service agreements and commission evidence are never included.

Hotel directory pages contain 20 hotels. Hotel workspaces load that hotel's bookings and payout history; larger histories may eventually need additional pagination. There is no payment-provider integration, automatic transfer, automatic deduction calculation, partial booking payout, or new finance role.

## Step 7 review and settlement statements

The deduction editor distinguishes Not reviewed, Enter amount, Confirmed ₹0 and Not applicable. It requires reasons, keeps blank inputs incomplete, parses decimal rupees exactly into integer paise, and shows the amount before deductions, total deductions and final hotel payment. Amounts that leave no positive payment are rejected by both the editor and assessment command. Commission is already accounted for and is not entered as another deduction.

Use **View statement** on a payout in admin or owner History. The standalone HTML page supports **Print / Save as PDF**, shows the saved hotel/payment/booking breakdown and itemised deductions, and identifies Prepared, Paid, Cancelled and accounting-review states. It is a settlement statement, not a tax invoice. Monetary figures come from immutable payout snapshots, not current settings. Missing historical figures show Not available; missing deduction assessments show Not reviewed. Internal reasons, payment notes, actor IDs, fingerprints and policy revisions are excluded from the statement. Existing administrative audit details remain in History.

Additional admin API: POST `/api/admin/payouts/preview?propertyId=<id>` with `{bookingIds}` returns a read-only review and `reviewFingerprint`. The existing prepare command accepts optional `expectedReviewFingerprint`; the updated UI always supplies it. Existing clients without that field retain the existing eligibility, reservation, assessment and minimum checks. Matching-fingerprint preparation is checked transactionally, and identical committed retries still replay their original result.

Statement HTML: GET `/api/admin/payouts/<payoutId>/statement?propertyId=<id>` and owner-only GET `/api/partner/properties/<propertyId>/payouts/<payoutId>/statement`. Both authorize access before rendering, use private/no-store responses, escape dynamic content, and expose the same statement allowlist. Printing hides controls, repeats table headings and supports multiple pages. There is no server-side PDF storage, new document-numbering scheme or financial migration.
