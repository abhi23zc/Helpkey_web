# Phase 3B — hotel invoices and credit notes

Settings → Invoice settings and the reservation Folio / Billing → Invoices panel share the existing Executive Reserve workspace. Cashiers see document history and printing inside their restricted financial workspace; receptionists and operational departments gain no invoice access. Receipts, billing statements, ordinary invoices, tax invoices and credit notes retain separate labels and purposes.

## Issuance and ownership

Owners/managers save revision-controlled, accountant-confirmed seller settings and explicitly review the billing recipient and source particulars. Booking contacts never become billing recipients automatically. Settings and document-details histories retain before/after values, actor and timestamp. Customer/guest ownership remains unchanged. Browser tab drafts survive refresh, query updates and errors. Pending issuance payloads and keys are stored before sending, including across a page reload, and remain frozen after an ambiguous outcome through later authorization, validation and rate-limit errors until an exact replay or transaction-confirmed uncommitted result resolves them.

Documents cover only the reservation pool. Original accommodation, individually referenced included extras and net billing correction families retain exact source amounts. Missing/contradictory source history, nonzero customer fees without supported document ownership, missing seller confirmation, unsupported tax particulars and unreconciled folios block issuance with correction guidance. Supplemental services remain separately visible in the folio and are not invoiced by this phase. No ownership follows merely from collecting money.

Accountant-confirmed document metadata records exact source amounts, taxable values, supported components/rates, quantity/unit, SAC, place of supply and provenance. It cannot change charges, taxes owed, payments or refunds. No automatic reversal of inclusive totals or allocation of aggregate taxes occurs. Existing original tax amounts, when available, must match documented original taxes. Missing original components are never filled from arithmetic guesses.

Issuance requires an idempotency key and a reviewed dependency digest covering seller, customer, booking, reconciled sources, claims and document history. A single Firestore transaction rechecks current access, operational activation, administrative restrictions, booking state and review, claims sources, reserves the next hotel/series/financial-year number, and creates the permanent snapshot, audit event and receipt. Booking balances and Today financial counts are unchanged. Independent requests cannot claim the same amounts; exact-key recovery returns the original result. Failure consumes no number.

Server hotel-local dates select April–March financial years. Full numbers use `series/YYZZ/counter`, within the 16-character permitted format. Separate invoice and credit-note series and starting numbers are chosen before first issuance and then locked. Sequences never reset within a financial year. No arbitrary date input is accepted. Historical printing reads saved seller/customer/tax/line/payment/signatory/footer fields exclusively; later payments or settings changes cannot rewrite a document. New positive charge amounts receive an additional invoice.

## Credit notes and boundaries

A credit note requires an existing invoice, a saved eligible folio reduction, reason, and explicit staff allocations to positive original invoice lines. Partial/repeated notes remain bounded by remaining reduction entitlement, original amount, taxable value and individual tax components. Family reductions use net compensation plus replacements, never gross compensations. Generic credits never imply a tax allocation: staff must explicitly record it. A generic credit included on the first invoice cannot be reused as a credit-note entitlement. Later generic reductions are documented using linked credit notes, independently from later positive-charge invoices.

Credit notes retain original document ID/number/date, source reduction, lines, reason, issuer and timestamp. They post no financial entries and initiate no refunds. “Credit note issued,” “Refund pending” and “Refund completed” are displayed separately from the existing reconciled money model. Folio correction/void commands transactionally reject changes that would reverse a documented reduction and require an unsupported debit note. Issued identity/tax corrections require external accountant review; snapshots cannot be edited or replaced by fabricated credits.

## Supported requirements

India first. Supported treatments are unregistered ordinary invoices without GST/ITC claims and registered taxable supplies with accountant-confirmed CGST/SGST or IGST and exact supported rounding. Hotel/accountant confirmation is required; registration obligations are never determined from size or booking value. Composition, exempt/zero-rated/export treatments, cess, reverse charge and foreign jurisdictions are blocked or outside the supported editor schema. IRP-required hotel or transaction documents are blocked; local printing is not an IRN substitute. Printed copies provide an authorized-signature space and require the hotel’s appropriate signing/review process. This implementation does not certify legal compliance, file returns or register an e-invoice.

Official references checked on 14 November 2026: [CBIC Rule 46 — tax invoices](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000136/1000001), [CBIC Rule 53 — credit/debit notes](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000144/1000001), [GSTN — e-invoice and IRN FAQs](https://www.gstn.org.in/faqs-category-details). Direct fetches intermittently failed; indexed official pages were available. These support numbering, original-document references and the IRN restriction; applicable legal treatment still requires accountant confirmation.
